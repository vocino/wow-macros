Aspect dance (230 chars)

#showtooltip [mod:shift] Aspect of the Cheetah; [mod:ctrl] Aspect of the Monkey; Aspect of the Hawk
/dismount [mounted]
/cast [mod:shift,outdoors,nocombat] Aspect of the Cheetah; [mod:ctrl] Aspect of the Monkey; Aspect of the Hawk

Plain press dismounts into Hawk. Shift gives Cheetah outdoors out of combat. Ctrl gives Monkey for melee. Why it works.

About these macros

Every macro here follows the rules in the macro wiki: one GCD spell per press, 255 characters max, allowlisted conditionals only.
